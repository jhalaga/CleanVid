YouTube captions hide or skip many swear words, and their timing is loose. CleanVid 2 listens to the audio itself. It runs speech recognition and word-level alignment on your computer, so it can mute each word to within a few hundredths of a second.

1

Hear

While the video loads, CleanVid reads the audio the YouTube player already buffers. It downloads nothing extra from YouTube.

2

Understand

Whisper transcribes the speech, then wav2vec2 pins every word to its exact moment.

3

Mute

Your word list is matched against the transcript, and each flagged word is faded out to silence (or bleeped) right on time.

Choose a model pack
